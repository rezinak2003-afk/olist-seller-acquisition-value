LIMITATIONS
- Seller-ID crosswalk is incomplete: 316/656 mature deals are unlinked. Their marketplace outcomes remain unknown.
- No channel-spend data is available, so CAC, ROI, payback and incremental lift cannot be calculated.
- Acquisition origin is observational and is not randomized.
- Seller `won_date` is a funnel close date, not a verified seller registration/go-live date.
- GMV is order-item price in BRL; it is not seller profit or Olist-recognized revenue.
- Both data sources are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; attribution and non-commercial/share-alike obligations must be observed. Do not redistribute raw CSVs or use the dataset commercially without checking the licence and securing any necessary permission.
